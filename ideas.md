# Vaani — Visual Direction

## Three Design Approaches Considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal Sanctuary | A quiet, technical listening environment where a mint waveform makes the invisible voice-to-answer pipeline tangible. | 0.07 |
| Evidence Ledger | A forensic, source-first workspace that treats every answer as a traceable research object. | 0.04 |
| Kinetic Relay | An editorial system that gives retrieval and response stages a fast, expressive cadence. | 0.08 |

## Chosen Direction — Signal Sanctuary

### Design Movement

**Nexus AI Intelligence**, interpreted as a restrained technical instrument rather than a conventional SaaS dashboard. The composition treats voice as a signal travelling through a precise, inspectable system.

### Core Principles

1. **Signal before spectacle:** The waveform is both identity and live functional feedback.
2. **Traceability as interface:** Sources, retrieval strategy, timing, and guardrail outcomes remain visible rather than hidden behind a chat bubble.
3. **Calibrated density:** Generous black space lets the active query and mint checkpoints stand out; telemetry is compact and editorial.
4. **Intentional restraint:** Surfaces use fine borders, small radii, restrained glow, and no decorative AI imagery.

### Color Philosophy

The warm near-black canvas (#030303) makes the system feel laboratory-grade and focused. Mint #40E79A is reserved for progress, verified grounding, and primary action; deep green #11A769 carries secondary state. #888888 and #A19FA9 provide quiet evidence hierarchy, while white is reserved for active linguistic content.

### Layout Paradigm

An **asymmetric signal console**. The hero begins as a wide, clear listening field, then resolves below into a staggered operating surface: a dominant live pipeline and answer region on the left, with an offset diagnostic rail for latency and grounding on the right. On narrow screens the rail moves below the answer without removing context.

### Signature Elements

1. Horizontally drifting, thin mint flow lines that turn into responsive mic bars during capture.
2. Hairline technical separators with monospace labels, sequence numbers, and completion timestamps.
3. Deliberate status glyphs: an outlined circle for waiting, a mint filled point for completed, and a split-ring state for retry.

### Interaction Philosophy

The interface should behave like a calm technical instrument: clicks acknowledge immediately, voice state changes modify the waveform, and each pipeline stage earns its visible completion state. Refusals are framed as verified system integrity, not failure.

### Animation

Use a slow 12–16 second waveform drift while idle, a live amplitude response during microphone capture, and compact 180–240ms opacity/transform transitions for state handoffs. Completed stages reveal sequentially at 50ms intervals. Only transforms and opacity animate; all non-essential animation respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Inter** carries display language, with 500–600 weight for the hero and response headings. **Geist** forms the reading layer at 16px/1.6. **JetBrains Mono** labels timing, confidence, source positions, and internal pipeline states in uppercase 11–12px with gentle tracking.

### Brand Essence

**Vaani is a voice-to-answer RAG console for teams who need every response to be inspectable, grounded, and fast.** Personality: **precise, composed, accountable**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are compact and declarative; microcopy names exactly what the system is doing; CTAs are verb-led and concrete. Example lines: “Speak. Retrieve. Verify.” and “No grounded context detected — answer withheld.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a bold circular microphone aperture crossed by three horizontal data-signal lines, rendered in mint on transparency. The wordmark uses spaced Inter Medium with the final “i” treated as a signal point.

### Signature Brand Color

**Vaani Mint — #40E79A.**
